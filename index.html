02 / 原则PrinciplesFIXED / NOT CONFIGURABLE
01

本地优先Local-first

数据留在你的机器上。能不联网就不联网。Data stays on your machine. Offline when it can.

02

不要账号No accounts

能不登录就不登录；没有账号系统。No account when it can be avoided. There is no account system.

03

一个人One person

设计、代码、打包、文档，都是一个人。Design, code, packaging and documentation are one person.

04

做完再发Finished before shipped

不拿没做完的东西占位。Nothing unfinished is used as filler.

03 / 工作室Studio

一个人的软件工作室。A one-person software studio.

Skana 做桌面和移动端的工具，偏好本地优先。界面安静，功能做完。DevSweep、Moment Bloom 和下一件，都按这个标准做。Skana makes desktop and mobile tools, with a preference for local-first. A quiet interface, a finished feature. DevSweep, Moment Bloom and the next one are all held to that standard.
